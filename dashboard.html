Top story · 11 May 2026 · EU Commission

EU Commission publishes DMA interoperability factsheet — Apple's 1 June deadline lands and Google's draft AI-access measures open for comment

The Commission released a factsheet detailing how DMA enforcement is delivering interoperability and data-portability improvements for smartphone users. Apple must roll out interoperability for notifications, proximity pairing and audio switching by 1 June 2026, while Google has until 13 May to respond to draft measures forcing it to open Android to third-party AI services. Apple and Google are also building a cross-OS device-transfer solution.

Comment deadline on Google Android AI interoperability measures: 13 May 2026

Highlighted in the Commission's 11 May factsheet: developers and providers have until 13 May 2026 to submit views on draft Article 6(7) measures requiring Google to give third-party AI services the same Android features Gemini gets — wake-word activation, contextual data access, and integration with device apps for task execution.
