# Prosus Digital Monitor

Live monitoring site for regulatory and competition-law developments relevant
to Prosus portfolio companies. Refreshed twice daily.

## Pages

- **`index.html`** — landing page, lets the visitor choose a monitor
- **`dashboard.html`** — Digital & Regulatory Monitor (AI, privacy, IP, launches, legal ops, trending)
- **`competition-law-monitor.html`** — Competition Law Monitor (antitrust + merger control)

## Hosting

Deployed via Netlify from this folder. Both ratings and the feedback form
flow into Netlify Forms (no PII collected). Updates are live within ~30 s of
a push to `main`.
